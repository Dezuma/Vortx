# Litigation Lightning Growth

The first release is B2B only. The goal is to sell early public-record discovery and alert workflows, not a consumer speculation product.

## Core Offer

Vortx sells Predictive Friction:

- Which entities are accumulating legal-record stress.
- Which records caused the score to move.
- Which sources are fresh, stale, or under review.
- Which watched entities crossed subscriber-defined thresholds.

## Buyers

- Debt collectors tracking counterparties before assets freeze.
- Law firms monitoring potential defendants, suppliers, or target accounts.
- Credit and risk teams watching customer/vendor distress.
- Short-biased research teams that need source-linked public records.
- Competitors doing stability and account-risk intelligence.

## Revenue Ladder

- `nebula`: Litigation Feed, limited searches, daily digest.
- `supernova`: Watchlists, entity timelines, exports, transparency drilldown.
- `galactic`: Premium alerts, higher limits, early delivery, Telegram/email/SMS routing.
- `custom`: Jurisdiction packs, enterprise onboarding, API, SLA, and research desk.

## Launch Loops

- Publish source transparency reports to build trust.
- Use anonymized sample timelines for demo pages.
- Offer narrow jurisdiction packs before broad national coverage.
- Sell custom watchlists as the premium wedge.
- Add correction workflows early to reduce false-match and defamation risk.

## Deferred

Prediction markets, public wagering, character combat, and consumer speculation are out of scope until data quality, legal review, and B2B revenue are proven.
